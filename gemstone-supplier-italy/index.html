A gemstone supplier in Milan and Italy

Natural Diam Srl is a gemstone supplier based in Milan, Via Giuseppe Mazzini 16. We supply natural diamonds and certified rubies and sapphires to jewelers, workshops, resellers, dealers and collectors across Italy.

We have offices in Milan, Mumbai and Bangkok, and reply directly from our office in Milan.

GIA and IGI certified natural diamonds

Certified diamonds come with a GIA or IGI report and each diamond is linked to its own report. We supply certified diamonds above 0.30 ct and perfectly calibrated melee up to 0.70 mm.

Our stones are fully traceable and responsibly sourced.

Certified rubies and sapphires from 2 carats

We offer certified natural rubies and sapphires from 2 carats, with Gübelin, SSEF, GRS and Christian Dunaigre reports. Origin and treatment are stated for every stone, and the catalogue separates unheated stones (No heat) from heated ones (Heated).

For colour we use the trade terms in English, such as Pigeon Blood and Royal Blue.
