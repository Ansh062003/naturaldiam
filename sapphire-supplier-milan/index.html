If you are looking for a supplier of sapphires in Milan or Italy, we work with certified stones from 2 carats. Every sapphire comes with a report from a recognised gemological laboratory, stating type, origin and treatment.

Certified sapphires: Gubelin, SSEF, GRS and Christian Dunaigre

We work with reports from the leading gemological laboratories: Gübelin, SSEF, GRS and Christian Dunaigre. The report is the foundation of any wholesale purchase: it confirms the stone is natural, states its origin and declares any treatment.

In the online catalogue we separate unheated sapphires (No heat) from heated ones (Heated), so you can compare stones with the right information in front of you.

Sapphire colour: Royal Blue and Cornflower Blue

We describe colour with the trade terms used internationally, such as Royal Blue and Cornflower Blue for blue sapphires. We keep them in English because they are the terms used on reports and by dealers worldwide.

Sapphires are not only blue: the catalogue includes other colours as well. If you need a specific shade, message us and we reply with the stones available.

How to read the treatment and origin of a sapphire

Heating is a common treatment for rubies and sapphires, and the report states it explicitly. An unheated stone and a heated stone can differ greatly in value, so the laboratory report is what establishes what you are buying.

That is why we never describe a stone in words alone: we show you the report. Ask for the report, photos and video of the stone you like, and compare origin, treatment and carat weight before you decide.
